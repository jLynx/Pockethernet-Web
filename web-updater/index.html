Firmware Updater

Install the official Pockethernet 2 firmware over USB.

Prepare your tester

  1. Switch the Pockethernet 2 off.
  2. Plug it into this computer or phone with a USB data cable.
  3. Select Update firmware and choose Pockethernet 2.

Keep the tester connected until the updater says it is safe to unplug.
